How Judging Works

On September 26th at the SJSU Student Union Ballroom (2nd Floor), judges will circulate through all project tables during the Demo Fair, spending 5–7 minutes with each team. They'll ask questions about your approach, technology choices, and impact. After the fair, judges deliberate and score each project using our standardized rubric. The Awards Ceremony begins at 2:40 PM.
