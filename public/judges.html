Meet the Judges

Our distinguished panel of judges brings decades of experience from Silicon Valley, academia, and the AI industry. They will evaluate your projects across all tracks.

How Judging Works

During the Demo Fair, judges will circulate through all project tables, spending 5–7 minutes with each team. They'll ask questions about your approach, technology choices, and impact. After the fair, judges deliberate and score each project using our standardized rubric.
